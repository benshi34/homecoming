# LinkedIn

Connections, positions, searches, reactions, invitations and LinkedIn's inferences about you.

Where: https://www.linkedin.com/mypreferences/d/download-my-data

1. Sign in to LinkedIn.

2. Choose "Download larger data archive", then Request archive.

3. The full archive arrives by email in about a day.

The download link usually comes in 12 hours, at most 2 days. The file: `Complete_LinkedInDataExport_<date>.zip (sometimes .zip.zip)`.
