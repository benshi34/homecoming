# Keep your Google activity

Google can auto-delete your search, YouTube and synced Chrome history: accounts made since mid-2020 do after 18 months (YouTube after 36).

Where: https://myactivity.google.com/activitycontrols

1. Open Activity controls and sign in with your own Google account.

2. Web & App Activity → Auto-delete: choose "Don't auto-delete activity".
   Also tick "Include Chrome history and activity", so Google keeps your Chrome history beyond Chrome's own 90 days.

3. YouTube History → Auto-delete: choose "Don't auto-delete activity".
