# ChatGPT

Every ChatGPT conversation.

Where: https://chatgpt.com/settings/data-controls

1. Sign in to your own ChatGPT account.

2. Settings → Data controls → Export data → Confirm export.

3. The email link expires after 24 hours. Download it soon after it arrives.

The download link usually comes in 1 hour, at most 1 day. It works for 1 days. The file: `<id>-<date>.zip with conversations.json`.
