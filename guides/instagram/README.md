# Instagram

Direct messages (both sides), likes, saves, follows, comments and searches.

Where: https://accountscenter.instagram.com/info_and_permissions/dyi/

1. Sign in to Instagram.

2. Export your information → Create export → pick your Instagram account → Export to device.

3. Leave Customize information on All available information. Date range: All time. Format: JSON. Media quality: Higher quality.
   Instagram defaults to Last year, HTML and Medium. Change all three.

4. Check the Notify email is one you read.

5. Start export. Enter your password again if asked.

The download link usually comes in 1 hour, at most 2 days. It works for 4 days. The file: `instagram-<username>-<date>-<id>.zip`.
