# X (Twitter)

Your posts (deleted ones too), likes, direct messages and Grok chats.

Where: https://x.com/settings/download_your_data

1. Sign in to X in your browser.

2. Settings → Your account → Download an archive of your data. Enter your password and the code X sends.

3. Request archive. X notifies you in a day or more. Download it from the same page.

The download link usually comes in 1 day, at most 4 days. The file: `twitter-<date>-<id>.zip`.
