# Spotify

Every song, podcast and audiobook you ever played, your playlists, library and searches, and what Spotify decided about you.

Where: https://www.spotify.com/account/privacy/

1. Sign in to Spotify.

2. Tick both Account data and Extended streaming history.
   Account data has your playlists, library, searches and the last year's plays. Extended streaming history has every play since you joined.

3. Request data, then confirm in the email Spotify sends.

4. They arrive separately, each in its own email: Account data in about 5 days, Extended streaming history in days, up to 30. Add each one when it comes.

The download link usually comes in 3 days, at most 30 days. The file: `my_spotify_data.zip, one for each part`.
