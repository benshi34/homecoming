# iPhone backup

Everything on the phone: full message history, calls, Health, Safari, WhatsApp, WeChat and app data.

1. Connect your iPhone to this Mac with a cable and unlock it.

2. Open Finder and select the iPhone in the sidebar. Trust this computer if asked.

3. Choose "Back up all of the data on your iPhone to this Mac".

4. Turn on "Encrypt local backup" and set a password you will remember.
   Unencrypted backups leave out call history, Health and Safari.

5. Click Back Up Now and keep the phone connected until it finishes.
