# iPhone notifications on this Mac

Services often say your data is ready with a notification on your iPhone. Forwarded to this Mac, the app can notice it and tell you.

1. On this Mac, open System Settings → Notifications.

2. Turn on "Allow notifications from iPhone".
   It comes with iPhone Mirroring: macOS 15 and iOS 18 or later, the same Apple Account on both, and not offered in the EU.

3. Choose which iPhone apps may show notifications here, or leave them all on.
