# Facebook and Messenger

Messenger conversations (both sides), friends, posts, groups, events and searches.

Where: https://accountscenter.facebook.com/info_and_permissions/dyi/

1. Sign in to Facebook.

2. Export your information → Create export → pick your Facebook profile → Export to device.

3. Leave Customize information on All available information excluding data logs. Date range: All time. Format: JSON. Media quality: Higher quality.
   Facebook defaults to Last year, HTML and Medium. Change all three.

4. Check the Notify email is one you read.

5. Start export. Facebook asks for your password here and again at download.

The download link usually comes in 1 hour, at most 2 days. It works for 4 days. The file: `facebook-<username>-<date>-<id>.zip`.

Not in it:
- End-to-end encrypted chats (the default since late 2023) are not in this export.
