# Full Disk Access

One permission unlocks Messages, Mail, Photos, Notes, Calendar, Reminders, call history, Safari and Screen Time.

1. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access.

2. Turn on Homecoming.
   If it isn't in the list, choose +, then pick Homecoming in Applications. macOS may ask to quit and reopen it: say yes, and the app carries on where it was.

3. Come back here and choose Check Again.
